## Read the guidance

1. Open the original Japanese [upstream skill](https://github.com/kociden/sakurai-skill/blob/main/skills/sakurai/SKILL.md)
   for game-production principles and its review procedures. Keep its wording unchanged.
2. Follow its [14 category references](https://github.com/kociden/sakurai-skill/tree/main/skills/sakurai/references)
   for the concrete numbers, procedures and work examples relevant to the request.
   Load only the needed categories. Do not install upstream tools or examples.
3. Report the sources actually read, relevant guidance and its limits. If a fetch
   fails, report the concrete error and URL. Retry when online or ask for an
   authorized local copy; do not invent missing guidance.

## Attribution and reliability

Upstream is an unofficial fan-made resource based on Masahiro Sakurai's YouTube
series [桜井政博のゲーム作るには / Masahiro Sakurai on Creating Games](https://www.youtube.com/@sora_sakurai_jp).
The series belongs to its copyright holders, Masahiro Sakurai and Sora Ltd.
Neither upstream nor this wrapper implies affiliation or endorsement.
The upstream content consists of AI summaries of automatic YouTube captions.
They may contain transcription errors and are not human-verified. They are not
verbatim statements by Sakurai. Check the original videos before relying on a
specific claim.
